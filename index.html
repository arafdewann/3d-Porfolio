About me

I'm more than one job title

Ask me what I do and the quick answer is operations and scheduling. It's true, and it fills most of my week. But it has never been the whole picture.

I've supervised security sites, written incident reports and stayed calm when a night got loud. I've done IT support, where the real skill is figuring out what someone needs rather than what they typed into the ticket. And I build things: websites, a blog platform with its own CMS, and small AI tools. My Computer Programming diploma from Seneca gave me the foundation, and my work keeps handing me problems worth solving with code.

  • OperationsKeeping people, sites and schedules lined up, across 50+ sites and 500+ staff.
  • Web developmentWebsites and a full content management system, from HTML and CSS to Node.js.
  • AI engineering and toolsChatbots and tools that take repetitive work off people's plates. This is where I'm growing the fastest.
  • IT supportWindows, hardware, networks and the patience to walk someone through it.
  • SecuritySupervision, reporting and fire alarm and security system monitoring.

None of these is a side hobby of the others. Security taught me to stay calm. Support taught me to listen. Operations showed me where the pain is. Code is how I fix it.

The best tool is the one built by someone who has felt the problem.
